# Flood audit, 2026-11-02

Played headless (screenshots: drop, race, map view, mid-race), read the code and the milestones
log, and checked against today's Brain training (comeback, foresight, drafting, anticipation, map
boredom). What is missing or thin, roughly in order of how much it costs the player.

## Clarity: the player can't read the game

1. **Map view (M) barely works.** It's a free orbit camera far above the island: the island fills
   about a tenth of the screen, and nothing marks the finish beach, rain clouds, storms or your
   own route. A map should answer "where am I, where is the beach, what's on the way".
2. **Pickups give about 6 s of warning** (Brain: anticipation). Clouds show only when they enter
   view; at 150-300 m/s that leaves no time to steer for one. They need markers at the screen edge.
3. **Choices swing a finish by about 28 s but can't be read** (Brain: foresight). Done in this
   pass: a route preview from the real movement rules (median error 9 m at 1 km) replaces the old
   1.2 km trace that stopped at the first rise in 47% of samples.
4. **The head is a crude blob** with a white rectangle on it; the river you play reads as a decal,
   not a surge of water.

## Fairness

5. **No comeback** (Brain: comeback). Ranks move in the pack (tau about 0.5), but 0 of 18 rivers in
   the last quarter at mid-race reached the top half. For a player who falls behind, the race ends
   early.
6. **Following a rival is a hidden penalty** (Brain: drafting): rival channels run 3-11% slower,
   and following the leader finished 8 of 24 against 20 of 24 for map reading. Nobody is told.

## Depth

7. **One skill.** Gouge is the only power move; the owner asked for "skills". Open forks T188
   (wind-up length) and T189 (cost scaling) shape any second one.
8. **No progression or memory between races.** Nothing is saved but the name and mute: no best
   time, no stats, no record of past races.
9. **Results screen is a bare list:** place, time, volume, lakes.

## Comfort

10. **No pause in solo, no settings** (volume, camera, controls). Mute is the only option.
11. **First race teaches through a footer line.** Contextual pops exist (blocked, wrong beach),
    but the basics (steer, the beach is the goal, lakes feed you) are small print at the bottom.

## Tech

12. **The desync fingerprint ignores terrain** (Brain G4): an off-path ground desync goes unseen.
13. **Bots ignore nearby rivals before using Gouge** (Brain G2).
14. **CI play tests are tied to software-render speed** (under 1 fps); timing budgets were raised
    today.

## Plan

Train first where the right answer isn't known (comeback lever, second skill, pickup warning),
then build in this order: map view, pickup markers, comeback lever, second skill, results with
best times and stats, pause and settings, first-race guidance, head visual, fingerprint over
terrain.
